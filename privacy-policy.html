Reflux IQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Reflux IQ ("Reflux IQ," "we," "us") is operated by Aaron Stanley, an individual developer based in California. This policy explains what information Reflux IQ collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact refluxiqsupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address (or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name/email those services share with us) and a password (stored securely by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see it in plain text).

Profile information. Username, birthdate (used only to confirm you're 13 or older — see "Children's privacy" below), and an optional profile photo.

Health-adjacent tracking data. Meals you log, their inflammation/symptom scores, ingredients you record, and any notes you add. This data is central to how the app works and is not medical data collected by a healthcare provider — see the in-app medical disclaimer.

Content you choose to share. If you post a meal to the community feed, that post (photo, food name, score, caption) becomes visible to other Reflux IQ users, along with your username and profile photo. You can hide a post from the feed at any time from My Meals; hiding a post removes it from other users' feeds but does not delete it from our systems until you delete the post or your account.

Points and leaderboard. Your accumulated points and username are visible to other users on the in-app leaderboard.

Contacts (optional). If you choose to use the "find friends" feature, the app reads phone numbers from your device's contacts and sends them to our backend to check which ones belong to registered Reflux IQ users. We do not store your full contact list — only matches are returned to you, and unmatched numbers are not retained. This feature is optional; you can decline contacts access entirely.

Reports you file. If you report another user's post, we store the post reported, the reason you selected, and your account ID, so we can review it.

Information about content you're reported for. If another user reports your content, that report (and the content itself) is reviewed by us to enforce these policies.

How we use this information

  • To operate the app: authenticate you, save your meal logs, show you the community feed, calculate scores and points.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, enforce blocks, remove content that violates our Terms of Service.
  • To communicate with you: account confirmation, password reset, and (once you finish onboarding) a welcome email. We do not send marketing email.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

Who we share information with

  • Supabase (our database, authentication, and file storage provider) — hosts all the data described above.
  • Resend (our transactional email provider) — used to deliver confirmation, password reset, and welcome emails; receives your email address only for that purpose.
  • Other Reflux IQ users see whatever you choose to post publicly (posts, username, profile photo, points) — this is inherent to how a social app works, not a third-party share.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs at this time. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Your choices and rights

  • Access/export: contact us and we'll provide a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Hiding content: you can hide any of your own posts from the feed without deleting them.
  • Blocking: you can block another user at any time; their content is then hidden from your feed.
  • California residents: you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. To exercise any of these rights, email refluxiqsupport@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Reflux IQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Onboarding requires a birthdate and blocks account creation for anyone under 13. If we learn a user is under 13, we will delete their account.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed according to the cascading deletion built into our database — meals, posts, points, and profile information are all removed together, with no manual step required on our end.

Security

We rely on Supabase's infrastructure, including Row Level Security policies that restrict each user's data access to their own records (and, for content you choose to share publicly, to other signed-in users). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever this policy changes, and for material changes we'll make a reasonable effort to notify you in-app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: refluxiqsupport@gmail.com.
